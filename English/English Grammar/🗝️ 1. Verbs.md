- Complete Intranstive Verb
	S+V (1형식)
	- Advertisement **pays**.
	- Her cousin **appeared** suddenly last week.
	- She **wirtes** to her parents at home once a week.
	- The sun has **risen** above the horizon.
	
- Incomplete Intranstive Verb
	- S+V+C (2형식)
	- He **is** a gentleman.
	- Her story **sounds** reasonable.
	- It **smells** delicious.
	- He **turned** pale at the news.
	- He **looks** younger than you.
	- The little station **looked** [dreary](<obsidian://open?vault=English Sense Atlas&file=dreary.md>)
	
- Complete Transtive Verb
	- S+V+O (3형식)
	- The [burglar](<obsidian://open?vault=English Sense Atlas&file=buglar.md>) **broke** the window.
	- He does not **like** cold water.
	- She **smiled** her thanks.
	- Can you **describe** the man to me?
	- He **[distinguished](<obsidian://open?vault=English Sense Atlas&file=distinguish>)** himself in literature.
	
- Dative Verb : 수여동사 (I.O~에게, D.O~를)
	- S+V+I.O+D.O (4형식)
	- I **read** my brother a fairy tale.
	- She **bought** me a gold watch.
	- He **asked** me a difficult question.
	- I have **promised** him a position.
	- She **cooked** her children vegetable soup.
	
		1. Indirect Object 앞에 to를 사용하여 3형식으로 변환
			- bring, give, lend, pass, pay, send, teach, tell등
			- 방향성과 전달 (to의 의미와 들어맞음)
			- 주어가 행위를 하는 순간 물건이나 정보가 상대에게 즉시 이동한다.
			- He **sent** me a present.
				-> He **sent** a present **to** me.
			- Please **give** me the salt.
				-> Please **give** the salt **to** me.
			- I **paid** him my respects.
				-> I **paid** my respects **to** him.
				
		2. Indirect Object 앞에 for를 사용하여 3형식으로 변환
			- buy, get, find, make 등 
			- 위함과 수고(for의 의미와 맞음)
			- 무언가를 구하거나 만드는 과정에 주어의 노력과 수고가 들어가 그 결과물을 상대에게 전달한다.
			- He **bought** Jenny a nice present.
				-> He **bought** a nice present **for** Jenny.
			- She **got** me a camera.
				-> She **got** a camera **for** me.
			- Will you **find** me my contact lens?
				-> Will you **find** my contact lens **for** me?
			- My father **made** me a kite.
				-> My father **made** a kite **for** me.
				
		3. Indirect Object앞에 of를 사용하여 3형식으로 변환
			- ask, inqure등
			- 상대방이라는 출처(from)로부터 답변이나 정보 요구를 끄집어내는 느낌
			- He **asked** me a question.
				-> He **asked** a question **of** me.
				
		4. to/for 판별 기준 : 상대가 그 자리에 없어도 혼자 먼저 할 수 있는가?
			- No : to (tell, give, pass등)
			- Yes : for (buy, make, cook등)
			
- Incomplete Transitive Verb
	- S+V+O+O.C (5형식)
	- Objective Complement : None, Adjective, to 부정사, 현재분사, 과거분사등
	- John **made** his son a pianist.
	- The court **declared** him guilty.
	- I **promised** her to come.
	- He **listened** to the band playing in the park.
	- I **had** my leg broken in the accident.
	- I **heard** my name called behind me.





# Comprehension Drill 1

1. Breakfast normally **consists** of eggs, some meat, toast, fruit, or fruit juice, and coffee. Lunch **is** likewise a light meal and **is** normally eaten away from home. People who work must either **take** lunch with them or **get** it near the place where they work. and children in school **take** sandwiches, fruit, and cookies along with them or **eat** in school cafeterias.

2. Doctor **encourage** people to have complete physical examinations regularly and not to wait until illness have become serious before having them treated. Everybody **believes** that "An ounce of prevention **is** worth a pound of cure." but not everybody **follows** this advice.

3. For thousands of years all wirting **was done** by hand. Not many people could **write**. and not many could **read** either. In most countries certain people **were especially trained** to do all necessary writing. The instruments they used **were** [primitive](<obsidian://open?vault=English Sense Atlas&file=primitive.md>) by modern standards.
