### 1. 요약문 받아쓰기
Listen carefully and write down the passage you hear.

Rome, a big empire a long time ago, stayed powerful mostly due to ==its== strong army. But the huge number of well-made roads also played ==an== important role. Rome's roads were more than 50,000 miles and continually expanded. These roads ==ensured== smooth operation of the empile. Romans used special concrete from volcanic ash and lime and made sure that rainwater would run off the middle to the sides. Some parts of the roads are still good enough to ==be== used today.

	2. marble

	1. to make it harder for people to walk on them.
	2. to create shortcuts for travelers.
	3. to allow rainwater to flow off

	1. dirt
	2. grass
	3. concrete
	4. wooden planks
